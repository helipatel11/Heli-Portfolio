Hi, I'm **Heli Patel**.

I'm a Computer Science graduate with a background in Information Technology and an interest in building intelligent applications and reliable cloud-native systems.

My engineering interests lie at the intersection of **MLOps, LLMOps, Cloud Engineering, and Kubernetes**. I enjoy working across the whole lifecycle of an application: designing and developing it, then deploying, monitoring, troubleshooting, and improving it.

I believe good engineering goes beyond making something work. It's about understanding why a particular approach makes sense, how different components interact, what can go wrong, and how to build systems that stay reliable as they evolve.

## Background

At Carleton University, I worked on research into **detecting anomalies in databases through user behaviour**. The idea was to spot insider threats that firewalls and access rules can't catch. My part was setting up MediaWiki and MySQL on a Linux VM, collecting the database logs, and pre-processing them into user interaction patterns that could be used to build behaviour profiles. That work is a big part of why I care about monitoring and about understanding what "normal" looks like for a system.

I also studied Human-Computer Interaction, including an expert usability evaluation of a large travel-booking site and a review of chatbot user experience. It taught me to always think about the person on the other side of the screen, and that a feature is only as good as how easy it is to use.

Before that, I worked as a software developer in an IT organization.
